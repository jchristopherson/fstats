public function doe_model_anova(mdl, x, y) result(anova)

Computes an analysis of variance (ANOVA) table for a DOE model.

The sums of squares are computed from the data as

with , , and .

NOTE: This routine is currently only partially complete. The degrees of freedom are fixed at 2 for the model and M - 3 for the residual (rather than being derived from the number of model parameters), and the p-value is fixed at 0.01 rather than being computed from the F-distribution. The total degrees of freedom is M - 1.
