public function doe_compare_models(mdl1, mdl2, x, y) result(comp)

Compares two DOE models using the same data set.

The residual sum of squares (RSS) of each model is computed by evaluating each model at x and comparing the result to y. Model 1 is treated as the "full" model, and model 2 as the "reduced" model.

NOTE: This routine is currently a placeholder. Only the two RSS values are computed from the data. The remaining outputs are fixed values that do not depend on the models or the data: the F-statistic is 1.5, the p-value is 0.1, no significant difference is reported, the degrees of freedom are M - 3 (full), M - 2 (reduced), and 1 (difference), and the conclusion text is constant.
